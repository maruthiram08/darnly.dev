How it works

No screenshots to drag. No explaining twice.

Today a bug costs you a screenshot, an app switch, a hunt for the right file and a paragraph of description. With Darnly it is three moves, and you never leave the page.

Step 1

Press the key

⌥ ⇧ B on any page, on localhost or your live site.

Step 2

Click the broken part

A red box follows your cursor. Arrow keys make it bigger or smaller. Esc cancels.

Step 3

Say what you expected

One sentence, then Enter. Got a picture of how it should look? Paste it in too. Then tell your agent "fix my bugs".

What your agent gets

The context a screenshot can't carry.

  • A screenshot with the element boxed in red and your note written on it.
  • The exact file and component that drew it, on your local project.
  • The element's selector, text, HTML and styles.
  • Console errors and failed requests from the last 60 seconds.
  • Clear rules: fix it, say what changed, ask if the note is vague.

Darnly for Mac

Bugs outside the browser, too.

A small menu bar app for Mac apps, the iOS Simulator and the Android Emulator. Same three moves, same inbox, so your agent already knows what to do.

  • Press ⌥ ⇧ D anywhere, click the broken part, type what you expected.
  • The element comes from macOS accessibility: its kind, label and accessibility id (testID in React Native), the best clue to the code.
  • Errors from the last 60 seconds: the Simulator, the Emulator (adb) or the Mac app's own log, secrets hidden.
  • Works on menu bar and notch apps. A welcome screen sets up the folder and permissions on first launch.
  • Agent in the cloud? Sign in once, and Mac bugs go to the same private cloud inbox as the Chrome extension.
Download for Mac Free · macOS 14 or later · signed and notarized by Apple
